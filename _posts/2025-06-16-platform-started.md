The Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) Curaçao has started building an online platform for reporting Curaçao's Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) indicators. The platform is based on Open SDG, an open-source tool used by national statistical offices around the world.

In this first step the site was set up for Curaçao and the first two indicators were published: 7.1.1 (proportion of the population with access to electricity) and 8.5.2 (unemployment rate).
