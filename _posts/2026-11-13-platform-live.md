Curaçao's SDG reporting platform is now publicly available. It covers all 17 goals and 251 indicators, and 32 indicators are reported online with charts, tables and metadata.

Each indicator page shows the latest available national data and its source. The reporting status page shows which indicators have data and which are still to be added. More indicators will be published as data become available.
