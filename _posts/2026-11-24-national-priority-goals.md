The platform now highlights Curaçao's six national priority goals: SDG 1 (No poverty), 3 (Good health and well-being), 4 (Quality education), 7 (Affordable and clean energy), 8 (Decent work and economic growth) and 14 (Life below water).

The pages for these goals carry a "National priority goal for Curaçao" label. The [reporting status]({{ '/reporting-status/' | relative_url }}) page now has a separate bar for the priority goals: 28 of their 86 indicators (33%) are reported online.
