This section brings together the national policies, legislation, and methodological references that relate to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Curaçao. It is intended for analysts, policy makers, and members of the public who want to understand the policy context behind the statistics published on this platform.

For each goal, a dedicated page describes the relevant national strategies, laws, responsible institutions, and methodological notes. The indicator pages show the data itself; the policy pages explain the framework around it.
